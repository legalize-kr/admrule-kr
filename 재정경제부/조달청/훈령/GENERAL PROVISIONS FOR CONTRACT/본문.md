# GENERAL PROVISIONS FOR CONTRACT

Article 1. SCOPE
  General Provisions for Contract shall apply to the procurement of Goods and/or services to be      purchased by the Public Procurement Service, Government of the Republic of Korea.

Article 2. DEFINITIONS AND MEANING
  The following terms and abbreviations used herein shall have the meanings as set forth below;
  ?“Government”meansthe Government of the Republic of Korea.
  ?“PPS” means the Public Procurement Service, Government of the Republic of Korea.
  ? “Supplier” means any person or organization that furnishes Goods under the contract.
  ? "Contracting Party" means the Bidder and the Supplier, collectively, who have entered into the contract with PPS.
  ? "Goods" means any raw materials, properties, products, equipment and supplies including, inter alia, any       parts, ancillary parts, components and spare parts, and services related thereto, including, any                installation, training, maintenance and repairs.
  ? "End User" means any public organization that actually uses the contracted Goods.
  ? “DTE” is a delivery term separate from Incoterms, which means delivered to End User' site; the              contractor shall be responsible for the delivery and installation of the Goods at the End User' site,           he/she has to bear the costs and risks which contain all the expenses for installation, transportation,         test operation, etc.
  ? “Month” means calendar month.
  ? Word importing the singular meaning suggests that the context includes the plural meaning and vice        versa.

Article 3. LANGUAGE OF DOCUMENTS
  All documents such as bids, transport documents, shipping advices, markings, drawings, instruction           manuals, bid bonds, performance bonds, letters, telexes, facsimiles, etc., in connection with the contract      under the General Provisions for Contract shall be made in English and/or Korean unless otherwise            authorized by PPS.
  In case where any conflict arises as to the interpretation of these two versions, the Korean version shall prevail.
  Other printed literature furnished by the Bidder may be written in another language provided that it is      accompanied by English and/or a Korean translation of its pertinent passages, in which case, for the        purposes of interpretation of the bid, the English and/or Korean translation shall govern.

Article 4. COMPUTATION OF TIME
  The number of days means calendar days including Saturdays, Sundays and holidays. However, in the case      where the expiry date falls on a Saturday, Sunday or holiday, the expiry date shall be extended to the         following business day.

Article 5. CONTRACT DOCUMENTS AND GOVERNING PRIORITY
  ?Contract documents shall consist of Contract Form, Bid Documents, Final Quotation, Invitation for Bids, Request for Proposal, General Provisions for Contract, General Instructions and Special Instructions.
  ?In the event of conflict between stipulations in the contract, the governing priority shall be;
     First: Contract Form
     Second: Bid Documents or Final Quotation,
     Third: Invitation for Bids, or Request for Proposal
     Forth :(1) Special Instructions,  (2) General Instructions, and (3) General Provisions for Contract, in the        order as stated.

Article 6. PERFORMANCE BOND

**①** In the case where the Overseas Supplier entered into the contract with PPS on the condition that the Overseas Supplier may deposit the performance bond, the Overseas Supplier or the beneficiary of the letter of credit shall establish a performance bond with the bank advising the issuance of the letter of credit, within twenty(20) days after the dispatching date of the credit advice by the above bank, in the form of irrevocable clean credit, cash, certified check or bank guarantee, in favor of PPS in the amount not less than ten percents(10%) of the contract value. The performance bond shall be valid three(3) months after the latest shipping date or any extension thereof, payable in the currencies of the contract and available for payment against PPS's certificate stating that the supplier and/or beneficiary has failed to comply with terms and conditions of the contract.

**②** In the case where a performance bond is established by a contractor who is residing in Korea; The contractor shall establish a performance bond in favor of PPS, before the conclusion of a contract, in the amount not less than ten percents(10%) of the contract value, in the form of cash, bank guarantee, securities by the laws of ROK, or surety bond issued by the Fidelity & Surety Companies in Korea, payable in Korean Won, and a contractor who is residing in Korea shall jointly take  responsibility for depositing the performance bond in the case where the Overseas Supplier under clause ① fails to deposit the performance bond.

**③** Performance bond shall be released upon instructions of PPS after satisfactory completion of the contract, or on its expiry date, whichever comes first

  ?However, if necessary, two or more contractors may jointly establish the performance bond without the permission of PPS.
?  In the case where the contract amount is less than US$20,000(twenty thousand dollars), PPS may exempt the contractor from the obligation to deposit a performance bond.

Article 7. GENERAL SPECIFICATIONS
  All Goods furnished under the contract shall be in compliance with the specifications stipulated in the contract, and shall be new and unused standard products, suitable in all respects for the services intended, manufacturer's most modern design, and equivalent or superior in all respects to products regularly supplied to the domestic trade by the manufacturer.
  Spare parts furnished shall be identical to original parts. Points not adequately described by the contract specifications shall be in accordance with the best commercial practices, and shall be sound, true to patterns, and free from injurious defects.
  All fittings necessary to complete the equipment shall be furnished by the contractor at no additional charge beyond the price specified in the contract, whether or not mentioned in the contract specifications. Except when otherwise provided in the contract, no payment for extras shall be made unless such extras and price thereof have been authorized by PPS.

Article 8. WARRANTY
  The contractor shall guarantee that, at the time of delivery, the Goods supplied under the contract will be free from any defects in material or workmanship and conform to the requirements of the contract notwithstanding the fact that the Government may have inspected and/or accepted the Goods.
  This guarantee shall continue until at least one year after the date of arrival, unless otherwise provided for in other terms and conditions. Notice of any such defect or non-conformance shall be given by PPS to the contractor within one year after the date of the arrival of the defective or nonconforming Goods.
  If required by PPS, within a reasonable amount of time after such notice, the contractor shall, with all possible haste, refurnish the defective or non-conforming Goods in accordance with Section 25. DELIVERY, all at the contractor's expense.
  This guarantee shall then continue for the refurnished or replaced Goods until one year after the date of arrival. If PPS requires refund instead of replacement or refurnishing of the defective or non-conforming Goods, the contractor shall repay such portion of the contract price of the Goods as is equitable in the circumstances within a reasonable amount of time following notice of the defects or non-conformance.

Article 9. VARIATION IN QUANTITY
  No variation in the quantity of any item called for by the contractor will be accepted unless such variation results from conditions of loading, shipping or packing, or allowance in manufacturing processes, and then only to the extent as specified in the contract.

Article 10. INSPECTION
  ? General Requirements
     If independent inspection is deemed necessary, PPS, within a reasonable amount of time from the effective date of the contract shall appoint an inspector to inspect, at contractor's expense the Goods prior to delivery. It shall be the contractor's responsibility to advise the inspector that the Goods is ready for inspection at least twenty(2O) days prior to the delivery.
     The contractor shall furnish the inspector with all reasonable facilities and assistance, including records of all inspection performance made by the contractor to enable the inspector to fulfill his obligation and responsibilities. Upon receipt of such advice, the inspector shall inspect the Goods without delay and will promptly and immediately issue a certificate of inspection for the Goods.
     The inspector shall have the authority to reject any Goods which are found to be defective in material or workmanship, or otherwise not in conformity with the requirements of the contract. In the event the inspector disapproves any Goods, the contractor may tender additional quantities for inspection and approval in lieu of the Goods disapproved, provided such tender is made during the term of the contract.
     In the event the inspector fails to inspect the Goods within twenty (2O) days after being advised that the Goods is ready for inspection, the contractor shall select, subject to PPS's approval, an independent inspector to inspect the Goods at the contractor's expense.
     At the discretion of PPS, PPS has a right to reject the entire quantity if any portion of the total Goods contracted is disapproved by the inspector.  The contractor shall be responsible that the Goods delivered to PPS is the same Goods as described in the certificate of inspection.
  ? Inspection Requirement
     Goods to be supplied under the contract shall be inspected and tested by the inspector appointed by PPS as to the following points, if applicable:
    (1)Physical and chemical properties of materials or elements incorporated
    (2)Workmanship and quality or construction, fabrication, and assembly of Goods.
    (3)Performance test and operating test of Goods.
    (4) Weight and quantity.
    (5)Packing conditions and markings.
    (6)Points to be further specified in the contract.
  ? Inspector's Mark
     All Goods inspected under the contract shall be stamped, stenciled or labeled with the inspector's emblem. If it is not practicable to mark the Goods with the inspector's emblem, the containers in which the Goods are packed shall be so marked. The size of the inspector's emblem may vary depending upon the size of the Goods, packages or shipping containers to be marked.

Article 11. DRAWINGS AND MANUFACTURER'S INSTRUCTIONS
  In the case where a contract requires approval of the drawings, within reasonable allowance of time required by PPS, the contractor shall forward by air mail to PPS such documents prepared in English as required below with the relevant contract number and other reference numbers on each copy, if applicable;
  1.General arrangement drawings.
  2.Installation drawings showing weight and dimension of the heaviest part/s.
  3.Electric wiring circuit diagrams and list of electrical specifications.
  4.Assembly and sub-assembly drawings.
  5.Lubrication system circuit diagrams.
  6.Instructions for assembly, installation, operation, and maintenance of the Goods.
  7\. Certified test reports.
  8\. Performance curves.
  9.List of spare parts included in the contract.
  Two(2) copies each of the above stated documents shall be bound, wrapped in waterproof paper and packed with each complete Goods shipment. All of the above mentioned documents shipped with the Goods shall be listed on the packing list for the package in which they are shipped.

Article 12.APPROVAL OF MANUFACTURING DRAWINGS
  In the case where a contract requires approval of the drawings prior to manufacture, the contractor shall    submit six(6) copies of detailed manufacturing drawings to PPS for approval within the period designated in the contract.
  When the contents of the manufacturing drawings are not in conformance with the contract specifications, request for amendment for the contract specifications shall be submitted to PPS for approval prior to the submission of manufacturing drawings.

Article 13. SUB-CONTRACT
  No sub-contract shall be made by the contractor with any party other than those who are described in the contract, as supplier or manufacturer for furnishing Goods contracted, without the written approval of PPS.

Article 14.PAINTING AND PROTECTIVE COATING
  If equipment to be furnished requires bright or machined surfaces to insure precision fit, such surfaces shall not be painted but shall be protected against corrosive action by a suitable protective coating.
  All other metal surfaces which are not to be normally painted shall be protected in the same manner. Protective coatings shall be of types and formulas which insure adequate protection to the surface, in transit and storage on route to final destination.
  All other Goods parts shall receive one coat of high quality paint before assembly and a second coat after assembly. Parts not accessible for painting after final assembly shall receive two coats of paint prior to assembly. No painting shall be performed prior to inspection unless specifically approved by PPS.

Article 15.PACKING AND IDENTIFICATION
  Except where otherwise specified in contract, all Goods furnished shall be packed in standard export packing in accordance with the contract number and name of the End User. Such packing must be sufficient to insure safe arrival at the destination, fully covering rough handling, salt spray, open storage, and exposure to salt atmosphere and any other overseas shipping hazard. If special packing is required in the specifications, a detailed description must be included in the bid.
  All parts and/or spare parts shipped unattached or separately from the basic equipment, must be legibly identified by tags, labels or inscriptions in indelible ink, strictly in accordance with the reference marks or parts numbers of the basic equipment or the assembly drawings.
  On wood, plywood, veneer, cases, barrels or kegs, all marks shall be applied by stencil with waterproof inks or oil paints, and these marks protected against weathering by clear water-resistant varnish.
  On metal or metal containers, marks shall be applied with water proof oil paints. Characters shall be legible and not less than one(l) inch high, unless restricted by the size of the packages. Packages and containers shall be marked on top and on at least two sides.
  When metal tags are used, they must be rustproof. Tin or tin- coated tags and uncoated steel tags are not considered rustproof and must not be used.
  When paper tags are used, they must be free of rope-stock, possess a minimum tearing resistance, and be double eyelet type fastened at each eyelet. After marks have been applied to the tags, the tags shall be coated with clear varnish. The packages shall be numbered in sequence to designate the package number and total number of the packages being shipped.

Article 16. PACKING LIST
  Contents of each shipping package shall be itemized on a detailed list showing the exact weight, extreme outside dimensions (length, width and height) of each container. If all containers are uniform in size and weight, the dimensions and weight of only one may be shown.
  One copy of the detailed packing list shall be enclosed in each package delivered. There shall also be enclosed in one package a master packing list summarizing and identifying each individual package which is part of the shipment. The box number in which the master packing list is contained should be shown on each packing list.
  On barrels, bags, drums or kegs where it is not feasible to place the packing list inside the container, all pertinent information is to be stenciled on the outside and will thus constitute a packing list.

Article 17. MARKING
   Marking shall consist of the following information in sequence and in frame commensurate with the size of the container:
  1\. Consignee : The Public Procurement Service, Government of the Republic of Korea.
  2\. Contract number.
  3\. End User.
  4\. Port of discharge.
  5\. Item number, if applicable, package number in sequence, and quantity per package.
  6\. Goods description.
    7.Net and gross weight, and cubic measurement.
    8.Shipper's marks and/or trade marks.
    9.Caution marks, if applicable.
  10\. Other marking required in the contract.

Article 18. INSTRUCTION MANUAL
  Whenever applicable, the contractor shall furnish printed instructions relating to installation, proper maintenance and repair, and part lists indicating various parts by name, number and diagram, with each unit of equipment.

Article 19. PATENTS
  The contractor shall hold PPS free from any liability of including costs and expenses for or on account of any patented or unpatented inventions, Goods, devices, or appliances manufactured or used in performance of this contract, including their use or disposal by or for PPS.

Article 20. PRICE CONTROL
  The contractor shall guarantee that the stated price does not exceed the regular price and that such price is not higher than that charged by other buyers similarly situated. Violation of this clause will entitle PPS to rescind the contract and/or to claim a refund for the excessive price paid.

Article 21. PAYMENT
  ? After the contract is made, PPS will apply to an authorized foreign exchange bank in Korea, for issuance of an irrevocable and without recourse; commercial letter of credit. Unless otherwise specified in the contract, the letter of credit shall be established in favor of the supplier in the amount of the total contract value, and shall be maintained for one(1) month after the latest shipping date specified in the letter of credit.
     Payment shall be made in U.S Dollars or other currency/ies designated in the contract upon presentation of the beneficiary's sighted draft accompanied by the following documents:
  1\. A full set of clean onboard ocean vessel or charter party Bills of Lading, marked : “freight prepaid” or “freight collect”, as the case may be, to port of destination, consigned to the Public Procurement Service, Government of the Republic of Korea.
    2.Beneficiary's detailed invoice showing Goods description, quantity, unit price, total price, and basis of delivery.
  3\. Inspection certificates with marking certificate and/or weight certificate.
  4\. Packing lists, if applicable.
    5.Insurance policies or certificates, if applicable.
  6\. Other documents required, if any.
  ? Payment for services shall be made upon PPS's instructions, in accordance with the contractual terms and conditions.
  ?However, payment for Goods and services supplied from within Korea shall be paid by the End User  directly to the supplier.

**④** In the event that the advance payment is made(prior to completion of contract implementation including installation and test operation) for the Goods or services supplied under the terms of DAP(DDP), the contractor shall establish a guarantee bond pursuant to the method stipulated in clause 6.

Article 22. SHIPPING READINESS
  In the case of a contract made on FOB berth terms, shipping readiness shall be cabled or advised with facsimile or with any other formal letter by the supplier to PPS 20 days prior to loading the cargo, containing such information as contract number, loading port, brief description of Goods to be shipped and invoice amount of shipment, PPS will provide vessels, and give notice 10 days in advance of the vessel's readiness to load. The supplier shall be accountable for demurrage incurred at the loading port due to the supplier's failure to make delivery during the contractual delivery period.

Article 23. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
  ?If the contractor fails to make delivery of Goods when due, PPS shall, at its discretion, impose the following penalty:
     To deduct, for each day of delay in delivery after the date specified, liquidated damages in the amount of zero point one five percent (0.15%) of the total value of the contract, or, if the contract has been, partially performed within the stipulated time, the total value of the unshipped portion thereof.
     In either case, PPS reserves the right to terminate the contract.

**②** In case that installation work is required, as stipulated in the Invitation, all installation work shall be completed at the End User's site within the designated time. The contractor shall submit a time table and dispatching schedule for the period of services as requested in the bid. If the work is not completed within the designated time, a delay charge of zero point one five percent (0.15%) of the contract amount per day shall be imposed. In this case the penalty for the delay in delivery [Clause23.a)] shall not be applied. This delay charge shall not apply, if the delay is not the responsibility of the contractor.

**③** In the case of Goods or services supplied under the terms of DAP(DDP), the liquidated damages shall be calculated on the basis of completion date of contract implementation including installation and test operation.

Article 24. INSURANCE
  In case of a contract made on CIF basis, an insurance policy or certificate shall cover Institute Cargo Clause (A), as designated in the contract for 110% of the invoice value, to be endorsed in blank, expressly state that claims are payable in Korea, in the currency of the contract and also indicate a claim settling agent in Korea.

Article 25. DELIVERY
  ? Delivery shall be effected upon presentation of a clean “onboard ocean vessel” or “party” bill of lading, “freight prepaid” or “freight collect” as the case may be, evidencing shipment in accordance with the contract delivery terms specified in the contract.
  ?Partial shipments, transshipments and on deck shipments are prohibited, unless otherwise stated in the contract.

Article 26. SHIPPING ADVICE
  ? Shipping advice shall be cabled or advised by facsimile or by any other formal letter to PPS prior to the onboard date of the Bill of Lading, containing such information as the contract number, loading port, brief description of the Goods shipped, name of vessel, ETD(estimated time of departure), ETA(estimated time of arrival), the invoice amount of shipment, and the name of claim settling agent in Korea, if insured.
  ? In case of air freight; Shipping advice shall be cabled or advised by facsimile or by any other formal letter to PPS before departing, containing such information as the contract number, airport of departure, airport of destination, carrier, name of flight, brief description of the Goods shipped, ETD, ETA, the invoice amount of shipment, and the name of claim settling agent in Korea, if insured.

Article 27. FREIGHT
  Actual freight shall only be paid to the supplier. In the case of the actual freight shown in the Bill of Lading or Air Waybill is higher than the freight specified in the contract, the excess freight shall not be paid by PPS.

Article 28. SUBMISSION OF TRANSPORT DOCUMENTS
  ? Supplier shall send each of the following documents in duplicate to the bank designated by PPS and a copy of the same to PPS.
    1.Charter-Party or liner booking contract, if applicable.
  2\. Clean onboard ocean vessel Bill of Lading or Air Waybill.
  3\. Supplier's detailed invoice.
  4\. Inspection & Marking certificate.
    5.Weight certificate, if applicable.
  6\. Packing list.
    7.Insurance policy or certificate, if applicable.
    8.Other documents required, if any.
  ? In case of Goods supplied from within Korea, the contractor shall submit one copy of certificate of acceptance issued by the End User as attached form of APPENDIX I and other documents required in the contract.

Article 29. DEFAULTS
  ? If a contractor refuses or fails to make deliveries of Goods conforming to the contract specifications within the time specified, or any extension thereof, or to perform faithfully any contractual conditions, or if the amount of liquidated damages reaches that of its performance bond, PPS, without prejudice to other rights of PPS resulting from the breach of contract, may, by written notice, rescind the contract or terminate the right of the contractor to proceed with any or all of the remaining part to be performed. In the event of any such rescission or termination, PPS may, without prejudice to its other rights resulting from the breach of contract, obtain similar Goods elsewhere.
 ? If the liquidated damages pursuant to Article 23 becomes equivalent to the amount of its performance bond pursuant to Article 6, Contracting Party have possibility to perform a contract, and it is acknowledged that contract shall be maintained, in this case, on the condition that the Contracting Party deposit an additional performance bond in an amount equivalent to 10% or more of the contract price for which contract has not been completed, and the contract may remain valid.
? In addition, the breach of contract will automatically result in the unconditional forfeiture of the contractor's performance bond without recourse. In any event, PPS reserves the right to recover from any money due or which may become due to the contractor, for the above mentioned excess costs and/or for any other losses to PPS resulting from the contractor's default or the breach of contract.

Article 30. FORCE MAJEURE
  The contractor shall not be charged with the liquidated damages prescribed in Section 23. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES and/or any excess costs or any other losses to PPS prescribed in Section 29. DEFAULTS, nor shall his performance bond be forfeited.
  ?When failure of the contractor in making suitable delivery is due to causes beyond his/her control and which could not reasonably have been anticipated, and were without his/her fault or neglect including, but not restricted to, acts of God or public enemy, acts of the government whether as contractor or sovereign, fires, floods, epidemics, quarantine res trictions, strikes, freight embargoes, and unusually severe weather, and delays and failure of a subcontractor due to any of such causes as above, only in case the subcontractor is specified in the contract in compliance with Section 13. SUB-Contract, and
  ? If the contractor notifies PPS in writing of causes of such failure not less than ten(10) days from the beginning thereof or such further period as PPS  may grant prior to date of the final settlement of the contract, PPS shall decide the facts and extent of failure, and its decision shall be final and conclusive, subject to the parties hereto holding right to appeal as provided in section.

Article 31.JURISDICTION OF THE LITIGATION AND ARBITRATION
  ?When legal proceedings are deemed to be necessary for the rights and obligation in connection with this contract, the First Trial Court for the concerned litigation shall be the court chosen by PPS among the competent courts by the law and one of the following courts:
  1\. Seoul Central District Court;
  2\. the Competent Court where the head office of PPS belongs.
  ? However, the dispute may be settled by arbitration in Korea the necessity of court proceedings, in accordance with the International Arbitration Rules of the Korean Commercial Arbitration Board and under the Laws of Korea only if an agreement is made between the parties to the dispute.

Article 32. REFUNDS
  Whenever the bidder receives from his/her supplier a discount, adjustment credit and /or refunds, he/her will notify promptly PPS's Administrator of the amount of money received and the date of receipt and will make refunds thereof to PPS.

Article 33. EXPENSES
  All the expenses for the fulfillment of the contractor's obligations incurred outside Korea, unless expressly stated otherwise in the contract, shall not exceed the contract.

Article 34. GOVERNING LAWS
  ? Unless otherwise specified in the Provisions, a contract shall be executed under the authority of laws, decrees and Governmental regulations of Republic of the Korea.
  ?The formation, validity, construction, and the performance of this contract shall be governed by the laws of the Republic of Korea.

Article 35.INTERPRETATION AND TRADE TERMS
  ?In the event of any disagreement between PPS and the contractor in the interpretation of the provisions of words used in the General Provisions for contract, and the terms and conditions of the contract, the interpretation by PPS shall govern.
  ? Unless otherwise specified expressly in the contract, it shall be assumed that trade terms are used in accordance with Incoterms (the most recent version as of the Bidding Announcement date).

Article 36. Review Period
  In accordance with 「Issued and administrative provisions for instructions and established rules」(Presidential directive No. 334), Administrator of the Public Procurement Service shall review adequacy every three years based on Jan. 1, 2017 (by the end of December of every three years), and if necessary, follow-up measures such as for improvement shall be taken.
